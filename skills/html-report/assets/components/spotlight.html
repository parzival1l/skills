The most useful mistake · P01

Reader v2 starts a new document at an attachment page

Page 8 ends with the word "Attachment". Page 9 starts with a new heading. Reader v2 starts a new document at that heading.

Rule: an attachment stays with its parent document. Pages 4 to 8 are one document. Reader v1 keeps them together.
